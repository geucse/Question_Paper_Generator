# Paper validator guide

As a validator you independently check an examination paper the COE assigned to you, before it can be approved and
printed. Each paper has **four validators**; each decision is recorded separately and is **final** once given.

Demo accounts: `validator1@portal.edu` … `validator5@portal.edu` / `Valid@12345`.

## Your workspace

**Validation queue** lists your assignments. The overview shows **Awaiting your decision**, **Decisions recorded** and
**Ended assignments**.

| In the queue | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Review** | A paper waiting for your decision; opens it |
| **Open** | A paper you have decided; shows your recorded decision |
| *Assignment ended* | The paper was regenerated, archived or you were replaced; nothing more to do |

You can open only papers you are assigned to, and only while the assignment is active.

## Reviewing a paper

Select **Review**. The **Paper review** page shows:

1. **Printed paper**: **Preview**, **Download** or **Print** the PDF **with answer key**, exactly as it will print
   (with a DRAFT watermark until it is final). Check the layout, numbering, instructions and page breaks here.
2. **Automated validation**: the CO and Bloom targets, what was achieved, and section checks. If the COE recorded an
   **override** of a target, it is shown with its reason.
3. **Sections and questions**: each question with its marks, type, difficulty, Bloom level, topic and CO codes, how it
   is numbered on the printed paper (for example *printed as Q2(a)*), and its model answer.

## Reporting an issue

Use **Report issue** to raise a specific problem: choose the question (listed by slot and printed number), and describe
the problem. The COE must resolve every open issue before final approval. Reporting an issue does not record your
decision.

## Recording your decision

Work through the **checklist** and tick each item you have confirmed:

| Item | Check |
|---|---|
| Question correctness | Every question and answer is correct |
| Syllabus relevance | Every question belongs to the course's syllabus |
| CO mapping | The CO codes fit the questions and the targets |
| Bloom levels | The levels fit the questions and the targets |
| Difficulty | The mix of difficulty is appropriate |
| Marks | Marks per question and the total are right |
| Formatting | The paper is formatted correctly |
| Numbering | Question numbers, sub-parts (a)/(b) and OR choices are right |
| Spelling / grammar | No language errors |
| Instructions | The instructions are clear and complete |
| Layout | Page breaks and tables print correctly |

Then choose:

- **Approve**: every checklist item is confirmed.
- **Changes required**: describe what must change in **Comments** (required).

Select the button to record it. **Your decision is final** for this round. If the COE regenerates the paper, you may be
assigned to the new revision and decide again.

## Independence

Each validator records their own decision under their own account; nobody else, including the COE, can record or
change it. Everything you do is audited.
